<div class="section"><div class="container narrow"><p class="eyebrow">About the Laboratory</p><h1>Building Materials and Construction Laboratory</h1><p class="lead">Kokushikan University · Architecture</p><p class="ja-note">建築材料施工研究室／国士舘大学 理工学部 建築学系</p>
<h2>Sustainable Building Materials and Digital Manufacturing Technologies</h2><p>Our research is grounded in building materials and construction. We investigate the use of natural materials, unutilized resources, and waste as building materials; building maintenance and renovation; digital fabrication; and VR-based construction education. We combine physical making with digital technologies to explore new approaches to building production.</p><p class="ja-note">天然素材・未利用資源・廃材の建材利用、建築物の維持保全・改修、デジタルファブリケーション、VRを用いた施工教育などを研究し、アナログな「ものつくり」とデジタル技術を組み合わせます。</p>
<h2>Research Background</h2><p>The laboratory was established in April 2012 and has been based in the Architecture program at Kokushikan University since FY2018. Its research follows a building-production perspective that treats materials, building methods, and construction as connected subjects.</p><p class="ja-note">研究室は2012年4月に発足し、2018年度より国士舘大学理工学部建築学系を拠点として研究・教育活動を行っています。建築材料・建築構法・建築施工を一体として捉える建築生産研究を基盤としています。</p>
<h2>Principal Investigator</h2><h3>Tatsuya INDEN / 位田 達哉</h3><p>Associate Professor, Architecture, School of Science and Engineering, Kokushikan University<br>Graduate School of Engineering, Construction Engineering</p><p><strong>Specialization:</strong> Building Materials and Construction</p><p class="ja-note">専門分野：建築材料・建築施工。天然素材・廃材の建材利用、建築改修、デジタルファブリケーション、VR建設現場教材などを研究しています。</p>
