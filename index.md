<section class="section"><div class="container"><p class="eyebrow">Research</p><h2>Building materials × construction × digital fabrication</h2><div class="grid">
<div class="card"><h3>Sustainable Building Materials</h3><p>Development and evaluation of building materials using volcanic ash, unused resources, wood-based materials, and waste materials.</p></div>
<div class="card"><h3>Digital Fabrication</h3><p>Application of 3D printing, 3D scanning, and parametric design to building materials, components, and construction.</p></div>
<div class="card"><h3>Building Maintenance & Renovation</h3><p>Maintenance, repair, seismic retrofit, and renovation of existing buildings from the perspective of materials and construction.</p></div>
<div class="card"><h3>Construction Education</h3><p>Experiential construction education, VR learning materials, work-efficiency measurement, and hands-on making.</p></div>
</div></div></section>
<section class="section"><div class="container"><p class="eyebrow">Laboratory</p><h2>Learning from the past to develop the future of building production</h2><p>Established in 2012, the laboratory has been based in the Architecture program at Kokushikan University since 2018. We connect materials, construction methods, measurement, digital technologies, and hands-on making to explore new approaches to building production.</p></div></section>
