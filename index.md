<h1>Building Materials and Construction Laboratory</h1>
<p class="lead">Sustainable Building Materials and Digital Manufacturing Technologies</p>
<p class="large">We study building materials and construction through sustainable resource use, digital fabrication, building maintenance and renovation, practical construction education, and interior production.</p>
<p class="ja-note">建築材料・建築施工を基盤に、未利用資源の建材利用、デジタルファブリケーション、既存建築物の維持保全、実践的な施工教育、インテリア制作を横断して研究しています。</p>

<section class="section"><div class="container"><p class="eyebrow">Research</p><h2>Five connected research areas</h2><p class="section-intro">Our research connects building materials and construction with resource circulation, digital manufacturing, existing-building maintenance, construction education, and making.</p><p class="ja-note">建築材料・建築施工を軸に、資源循環、デジタル製造、既存建築の維持保全、施工教育、ものづくりを横断して研究します。</p>
<div class="grid">
<div class="card"><span class="number">01</span><h3>Sustainable Building Materials</h3><p>Development and evaluation of building materials using volcanic pyroclastic materials, unutilized resources, wood-based materials, and waste.</p><p class="ja-note">火山砕屑物、未利用資源、木質材料、廃棄物等を活用した建築材料の開発・評価。</p></div>
<div class="card"><span class="number">02</span><h3>Digital Fabrication</h3><p>Architectural applications of 3D printing, 3D scanning, parametric design, and digital manufacturing.</p><p class="ja-note">3Dプリンティング、3Dスキャニング、パラメトリックデザイン等の建築応用。</p></div>
<div class="card"><span class="number">03</span><h3>Building Maintenance &amp; Renovation</h3><p>Long-term use, repair, renovation, and seismic upgrading of existing buildings.</p><p class="ja-note">既存建築ストックの長寿命化、改修、補修、耐震補強。</p></div>
<div class="card"><span class="number">04</span><h3>Construction Education</h3><p>Practical approaches to learning construction through experiments, hands-on work, VR, and field-based activities.</p><p class="ja-note">実験、施工実習、VR、現場型活動を通じた実践的な建築教育。</p></div>
<div class="card"><span class="number">05</span><h3>Interior Materials &amp; Production</h3><p>Interior materials, furniture, and production methods using construction waste, natural resources, and technologies from other fields.</p><p class="ja-note">建設廃材、天然資源、異分野技術を活用した内装材料・家具・製作。</p></div>
</div></div></section>
<section class="section section-muted"><div class="container"><p class="eyebrow">Research Approach</p><h2>From material evidence to digital production</h2><p class="section-intro">We connect experimental evidence on material properties with construction, renovation, fabrication, and education. Digital methods such as 3D measurement, VR, and data analysis are used where they contribute to understanding or improving building production.</p><p class="ja-note">材料の性質を実験・計測によって捉え、施工・改修・製作・教育へ接続します。3D計測、VR、データ分析などは、建築生産の理解や改善に有効な研究手法として組み合わせます。</p><div class="highlight-grid"><div><span class="tag">Measure</span><strong>Experiment &amp; measurement</strong><p class="ja-note">材料性能、環境性能、施工・作業の実態を実験・計測で評価。</p></div><div><span class="tag">Make</span><strong>Fabrication &amp; construction</strong><p class="ja-note">材料開発から部材製作、改修、施工教育まで実物をつくるプロセスを研究。</p></div><div><span class="tag">Analyze</span><strong>Data &amp; digital methods</strong><p class="ja-note">3D計測、VR、データ分析などを用いて設計・製造方法を改善。</p></div></div></div></section>
