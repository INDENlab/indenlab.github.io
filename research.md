<div class="section"><div class="container"><p class="eyebrow">Research</p><h1>Research Areas</h1><p class="section-intro">The laboratory studies building production from materials to construction, combining experimental research, fabrication, field practice, and digital methods.</p><p class="ja-note">建築材料から建築施工までを対象に、実験、製作、現場実践、デジタル技術を組み合わせて研究します。</p><div class="grid">
<div class="card"><h3>01 · Sustainable Building Materials</h3><p>Development and evaluation of building materials using unutilized resources, volcanic pyroclastic materials, wood-based materials, and waste.</p><p class="ja-note">未利用資源・火山砕屑物・木質材料・廃棄物等の建築材料としての有効活用。</p><ul><li>Volcanic pyroclastic materials for interior and spray-applied building materials</li><li>3D-printed sustainable building materials</li><li>High-strength compressed wood through delignification</li><li>Application of new materials and construction methods</li></ul></div>
<div class="card"><h3>02 · Building Maintenance &amp; Renovation</h3><p>Long-term use and improvement of existing building stock through maintenance, repair, renovation, and seismic upgrading.</p><p class="ja-note">既存建築ストックの長寿命化、改修、補修、耐震補強。</p><ul><li>Digital fabrication for repair</li><li>Renovation design and construction</li><li>Preventive maintenance of reinforced concrete</li><li>Low-cost seismic upgrading</li></ul></div>
<div class="card"><h3>03 · Digital Fabrication</h3><p>Application of 3D printing, 3D scanning, parametric design, and digital manufacturing to building materials and construction.</p><p class="ja-note">3Dプリンティング、3Dスキャニング、パラメトリックデザイン等の建築応用。</p><ul><li>3D-printed building materials</li><li>3D-printed sashes and building components</li><li>3D surveying</li><li>Digital fabrication for renovation</li></ul></div>
<div class="card"><h3>04 · Construction Education</h3><p>Methods for learning construction through experience, hands-on production, VR, and field-based activities.</p><p class="ja-note">経験的な建築施工を大学教育で学ぶ方法論と実践的なものづくり教育。</p><ul><li>Student work efficiency</li><li>Experiential construction education</li><li>VR construction simulators</li><li>Regional and existing-building projects</li></ul></div>
<div class="card"><h3>05 · Interior Materials &amp; Production</h3><p>New interior materials, furniture, and production methods using technologies from other industries and unutilized resources.</p><p class="ja-note">他産業の技術や未利用資源を応用した内装材料・家具・製作技術。</p><ul><li>Furniture using construction waste</li><li>Electrostatic coating for interior finishes</li><li>Natural unutilized resources in architecture</li></ul></div>
</div></div></div>
