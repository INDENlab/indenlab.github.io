<div class="section"><div class="container"><p class="eyebrow">About the Laboratory</p><h1>Building Materials and Construction Laboratory</h1>
<h2>Researching building materials and construction through tradition and innovation</h2>
<p>Established in April 2012, the laboratory has been based in the Architecture program at Kokushikan University since 2018. Its academic lineage is rooted in Waseda architecture and the study of building materials, building systems, and construction.</p>
<p>Our research combines sustainable use of natural and unused resources, waste-derived building materials, building renovation, interior production, digital fabrication, and VR-based construction education.</p>
<h2>Principal Investigator</h2><h3>Tatsuya INDEN</h3><p>Associate Professor, Architecture, Kokushikan University. His field is building materials and construction. His research combines physical making with digital technologies including VR, 3D scanning, and 3D printing.</p>
