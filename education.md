<div class="section"><div class="container narrow"><p class="eyebrow">Education</p><h1>Teaching &amp; Practical Education</h1><p class="section-intro">Teaching connects building materials and construction with experiments, surveying, construction planning, site management, ICT, and VR. The courses below are examples of teaching activities and are not intended as a complete current-year course catalogue.</p><p class="ja-note">建築材料・施工を、実験、測量、施工計画、現場管理、ICT・VRなどを通して実践的に学ぶ教育です。以下は担当・実施してきた教育内容の例です。</p><div class="grid">
<div class="card"><span class="number">01</span><h3>Experiencing the Mechanical Properties of Building Materials</h3><p>Students test concrete, steel, and timber in accordance with JIS-related procedures and learn material properties, testing equipment, and calculations.</p><p class="ja-note">コンクリート、鉄鋼材料、木材を対象に試験を行い、材料特性・試験方法・計算法等を学ぶ。</p><p class="meta">Kokushikan University Architecture · 2nd year · Spring</p></div>
<div class="card"><span class="number">02</span><h3>Understanding the Process of Building Construction</h3><p>Construction sequence, ground conditions, structural work, contractor roles, and site management are studied as a whole.</p><p class="ja-note">建築工事の順序、地盤、構造体工事、施工者の役割、施工管理者の仕事を学ぶ。</p><p class="meta">Kokushikan University Architecture · 2nd year · Autumn</p></div>
<div class="card"><span class="number">03</span><h3>QCDSE in Construction</h3><p>Quality, Cost, Delivery, Safety, and Environment are used to understand major construction work and site management.</p><p class="ja-note">Quality、Cost、Delivery、Safety、Environmentを軸に現場管理を学ぶ。</p><p class="meta">Kokushikan University Architecture · 3rd year · Spring</p></div>
<div class="card"><span class="number">04</span><h3>Practical Learning of Materials and Construction</h3><p>Building performance evaluation, leveling, angle and distance measurement, 3D surveying, construction drawings, and section details are studied.</p><p class="ja-note">建築性能評価、測量、3D測量、施工図、矩計図等を実務に近い形で学ぶ。</p><p class="meta">Kokushikan University Architecture · 3rd year · Autumn</p></div>
<div class="card"><span class="number">05</span><h3>Material Design and Construction Planning from a Site Manager's Perspective</h3><p>Students examine how construction planning affects quality, safety, cost, and schedule.</p><p class="ja-note">施工計画が品質、安全、コスト、工程に与える影響を現場管理者の視点から学ぶ。</p><p class="meta">Kokushikan University Architecture · 4th year · Spring</p></div>
<div class="card"><span class="number">06</span><h3>Learning from the Leading Edge</h3><p>New materials, analysis and evaluation technologies, ICT, BIM, VR, and management are used to explore emerging areas of construction engineering.</p><p class="ja-note">新材料、分析・評価技術、ICT、BIM、VR、マネジメント等を題材に建設工学の新領域を学ぶ。</p><p class="meta">Kokushikan University Graduate School · Construction Engineering</p></div>
<div class="card"><span class="number">07</span><h3>Experiencing the Properties of Major Structural Materials</h3><p>Material testing, quality control, data organization, discussion, and reporting develop practical understanding of building materials.</p><p class="ja-note">材料試験、品質管理、データ整理、考察、報告書作成を通じて建築材料を学ぶ。</p><p class="meta">Waseda University Architecture · 2nd year · Spring</p></div>
</div></div></div>
